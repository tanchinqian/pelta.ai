3.0 Rules & Regulation
3.0 Rules & Regulation
Each team must have 2 - 4 members in total. 
This hackathon is open ONLY to undergraduates
currently enrolled in any university or college in Malaysia. 
Teams can consist of members from different
universities/colleges. 
However, each team must designate a leader who is
affiliated with the university/college the majority of team
members belong to. The team will then be considered
representatives of that university/college. 
Individuals in privileged roles, such as organizers, judges,
mentors, and partner representatives, are not eligible to
participate.

3.0 Rules & Regulation
3.0 Rules & Regulation
Data Usage & Consent
The personal data of the applicants/Participants will be
used according to the Malaysia Personal Data Protection
Act 2010. By registering, the Participants agree that his/her
personal data may be stored and used by the Hack Attack
3.0 Organiser within the course and for the purpose of the
organisation to carry out HackAttack 3.0. 
This consent can be revoked at any time whereupon the
registration for the Hack Attack 3.0 will be automatically
cancelled. Stored personal data will be deleted upon
revocation or at the latest after the end of Hack Attack 3.0. 
Participants' personal data (such as Participant
identification) may be shared with event sponsors for
legitimate purposes, but consent can be revoked at any
time. 
Participants agree that any personal information or
pictures taken within the course of Hack Attack 3.0 may be
used for future promotional, marketing, publicity and
recruitment purposes without any claim to remuneration.

5.0 Preliminary Details
5.0 Preliminary Details
Participants must submit the following materials for the
Preliminary Round: 
1.Proposal [link for proposal template] 
a.Participants must follow the given template. 
b.Additional sections may be included if necessary. 
c.The proposal must not exceed 10 pages (excluding
the appendix). 
d.The proposal file cannot exceed 10MB.
Submission must be in docx. format. 
2.Presentation video 
a.The video must not exceed 5 minutes in length. 
b.The video should provide a clear project overview,
including its objectives and potential impact. 
c.The video should showcase the prototype and
demonstrate its functionality. 
d.Ensure the video is structured and visually clear. 
e.Submission must be in YouTube link format (set
to Unlisted or Public for judge access)

6.0 FAQs
6.0 FAQs
1) Can I work on more than one case studies?
No, each team can only work on one case study
2)  Can I copy existing code from any source?
You can have a boilerplate code to assist you in the
development. However, the idea of your solution must be
unique, and your team may face disqualification if found
of any plagiarism
3)  How do I contact the organisers for questions?
Feel free to reach out through our WhatsApp communiity
4) Are there any restrictions on the tech stack we can use?
No, there are no restriction on the tech stack you can use.
5) How will the projects be judges?
Projects wiill be assessed in groups based on predefined
judging criteria to ensure a fair and objective evualation
process
